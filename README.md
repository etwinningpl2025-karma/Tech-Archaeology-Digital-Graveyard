## 📌 About the Project

**Tech-Archaeology: Upcycling the Digital Graveyard** is an international eTwinning collaborative project aimed at raising awareness about electronic waste (e-waste), digital consumption habits, and environmental sustainability.

Through hands-on upcycling, creative engineering, and digital tools, students explore the history of tech artifacts, disassemble unusable devices, and repurpose them into new, functional, or artistic creations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* ♻️ **Environmental Awareness:** Educate students on e-waste management and circular economy concepts.
* 🔧 **Hands-on Learning:** Encourage STEAM (Science, Tech, Engineering, Art, Math) skills through upcycling and repairing.
* 🌐 **Digital Literacy:** Utilize digital platforms to document, present, and collaborate globally.
* 🤝 **International Collaboration:** Work with partner schools across Europe to share good practices and creative solutions.

## 🗓️ Timeline & Key Activities

* **Phase 1: Discovery & Research** 
  * Introduction of partner teams and baseline survey on personal tech usage.
* **Phase 2: Digital Archeology & Exploration**
  * Cataloging "retired" electronic devices and understanding their component anatomy.
* **Phase 3: Upcycling & Innovation**
  * Practical workshops: turning e-waste into art, dynamic tools, or functional home accessories.
* **Phase 4: Exhibition & Dissemination**
  * Virtual gallery / e-magazine showcase of final products and project evaluation.
